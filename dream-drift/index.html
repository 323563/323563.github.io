Gesture control

Race with your hands

Your webcam turns your hands into the controller. Sit about an arm's length away, with both hands visible.

🛞SteerHold an invisible wheel with both fists and turn it
🖐️BoostOpen both hands, even while you steer
👇BrakeLower both fists toward your lap
🙌JumpLift both fists up a hand’s width
🙈PauseTake your hands out of view

In the menu, point with your finger and pinch (or hover for a moment) to pick. Smile big and your racer cheers!

🔒 Everything runs on this computer. No video leaves the browser.
